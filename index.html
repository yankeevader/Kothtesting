    Rules

    Setup & Turn

    Perform exactly one action: Place, Move, or Jump.

    Place

    Place left/right on the same ring.

    Move

    Move one connected space. Inward requires friendly support.

    Jump

    Jump two spaces. Inward jumps require additional support.

    Convert

    Adjacent connected occupied tokens flip to the active token's color.

    How to Win

    The game ends when a player ends their turn with a token in the center or has no legal Place, Move, or Jump. The player controlling the most tokens when the game ends wins.
